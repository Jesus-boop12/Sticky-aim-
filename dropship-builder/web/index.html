How to put it online and take payments
  1. Click Download store and unzip it.
  2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder onto the page. Your store is live, free.
  3. Create a Stripe Payment Link for your product at your selling price, or use your PayPal.me name, and paste it into the Checkout tab. Then download and upload again.
  4. When an order comes in, buy the product from your supplier using the customer's address and email them the tracking number.
